# Gipoteza FUM

Gipoteza FUM - proveryayemoye predpolozheniye [FUM](FUM.md) o svyazi, prichine, mekhanizme, ogranichenii ili ozhidayemom rezuljtate, kotoroye yesjhyo ne imeyet dostatochnoj proverki, chtobyi schitatjsya podtverzhdyonnyim znaniyem.

Gipoteza FUM dolzhna byitj oformlena tak, chtobyi yeyo mozhno byilo proveritj: s oblastjyu primenimosti, ozhidayemyimi nablyudeniyami, vozmozhnyimi usloviyami oproverzheniya i svyazjyu s [eksperimentom FUM](eksperiment-FUM.md). Posle pervichnyikh uspeshnyikh proverok ona mozhet statj [siljnyim predpolozheniyem FUM](siljnoye-predpolozheniye-FUM.md), no bez vosproizvedeniya drugimi [FUM-uzlami](FUM-uzel.md) ne dolzhna schitatjsya [otkryitiyem FUM](otkryitiye-FUM.md).
