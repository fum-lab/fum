# FUM

FUM - abbreviatura latinskimi bukvami ot formulyi `fraktaljnyij uzel myishleniya`; po-russki - [fraktaljnyij uzel myishleniya](fraktaljnyij-uzel-myishleniya.md).

V proyekte FUM razrabatyivayetsya kak otkryityij agent sleduyusjhego pokoleniya: sistema s proveryayemoj [pamyatjyu](pamyatj-FUM.md), sobstvennyimi [agentskimi ciklami](agentskij-cikl.md), sposobnostjyu razvivatjsya, vesti [nauchnyiye issledovaniya](nauchnoye-issledovaniye-FUM.md), stavitj [eksperimentyi](eksperiment-FUM.md), oformlyatj [otkryitiya](otkryitiye-FUM.md), obmenivatjsya [narabotkami](narabotka.md) i v dolgosrochnom gorizonte uchastvovatj v sozdanii sobstvennoj sleduyusjhej versii.

V boleye shirokom smyisle FUM opisyivayet popyitku smodelirovatj uzhe nablyudayemuyu fraktaljno-evolyucionnuyu organizaciyu myishleniya, zhizni, socialjnyikh uzlov i civilizacionnyikh struktur, a zatem sozdatj dlya neyo inzhenernoye voplosjheniye na [kremniyevom substrate FUM](kremniyevyij-substrat-FUM.md).

V arkhitekturnom smyisle FUM opisyivayet ne toljko sam [uzel myishleniya](FUM-uzel.md), no i [interfejs FUM-uzla](interfejs-FUM-uzla.md): vnutrennyuyu storonu, cherez kotoruyu uzel nablyudayet i organizuyet svoi sostoyaniya, i vneshnyuyu storonu, cherez kotoruyu on vzaimodejstvuyet s poljzovatelem, drugimi uzlami, servisami i sredoj.

[Otkryitostj FUM](otkryitostj-FUM.md) yavlyayetsya chastjyu etogo opredeleniya: bazovaya pamyatj, trebovaniya i arkhitekturnyiye resheniya dolzhnyi byitj proveryayemyimi i nasleduyemyimi, potomu chto godnyij II-agent v predele dolzhen byitj sposoben razvivatj i pisatj samogo sebya.
