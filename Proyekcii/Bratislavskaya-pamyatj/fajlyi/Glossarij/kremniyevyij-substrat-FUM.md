# Kremniyevyij substrat FUM

Kremniyevyij substrat FUM - apparatno-programmnaya baza na kremniyevoj vyichisliteljnoj tekhnike, na kotoroj [FUM](FUM.md) dolzhen organizovyivatj vyichisleniya razuma kak upravlyayemyij, proveryayemyij i nasleduyemyij [FUM-uzel](FUM-uzel.md).

Termin podchyorkivayet inzhenernyij nositelj blizhajshego voplosjheniya [FUM](FUM.md), a ne ontologicheskuyu granicu myishleniya. [FUM](FUM.md) issleduyet principyi, nablyudayemyiye na raznyikh urovnyakh organizacii, no prakticheskaya realizaciya dolzhna perevoditj ikh v formu CPU, GPU, pamyati, nakopitelej, lokaljnyikh LLM, runtime, instrumentov, trass, proverok i [urovnej dostupa](urovenj-dostupa.md).

Kremniyevyij substrat otlichayetsya ot prosto "mosjhnogo kompjyutera": on dolzhen imetj opisannoye naznacheniye, kartu sootvetstviya mezhdu nizhnim apparatno-programmnyim sloyem i verkhnimi smyislovyimi urovnyami [FUM](FUM.md), vosproizvodimyiye proverki, ogranicheniya, otkaznyiye rezhimyi i svyazj s [pamyatjyu FUM](pamyatj-FUM.md).
