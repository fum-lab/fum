DNV-ST-C502 Offshore concrete structures

Standard

Edition 2018-02 - Amended 2101-10

SHARE:

This standard (ST) provides principles, technical requirements and guidelines for the design, construction and in-service inspection of offshore concrete structures. The concrete structures may be floating or ground-supported structures.
